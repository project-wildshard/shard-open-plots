- `pnpm dlx @wildshard/cli upgrade 0.8.0`: the SDK and cli at 0.8.0, format 0.80.0 (engine 0.1.96); no manual step.
  The hero re-stamped its content hash; the curated art is unchanged.
- Sixteen targets in `src/blender/targets.json` named the SDK's `glb.py` by its monorepo path,
  `scripts/blender/lib/glb.py`; they name it by its installed path, `node_modules/@wildshard/sdk/...`, and
  `check-model-sources.mjs` passes (30 targets, 58 GLBs).
- `kitlib.py` and `model_source.py` looked for `glb.py` in a `src/blender/lib/` this repo never had. They read it from
  `$SDK` (build.sh exports it), else the installed SDK.
